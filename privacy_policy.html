Privacy Policy for movieClipPosting

Last updated: September 2, 2026

movieClipPosting ("we", "us", or "our") is an application used to automate the publishing of video and other content to Facebook Pages and, where enabled, other supported social media services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, movieClipPosting may process:

  • Facebook account or Page information needed to connect and publish content.
  • Access tokens and authentication information required to communicate with Meta services.
  • Video files, captions, titles, descriptions, and other content that you choose to publish.
  • Basic technical information required to operate, troubleshoot, and secure the application.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only as necessary to operate movieClipPosting, including to:

  • Authenticate and connect your supported social media account or Page.
  • Upload and publish content at your request.
  • Track publishing status and troubleshoot failed uploads.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the application.

3. Facebook and Meta Information

If you connect a Facebook account or Page, movieClipPosting may access information and permissions provided through Meta's APIs. We use this information only for the functions you authorize, such as publishing content to a Page.

We do not sell Facebook information or use it for purposes unrelated to the functionality of movieClipPosting.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, or disclosed when required by law. Content that you instruct movieClipPosting to publish to a public social media Page may be publicly accessible on that platform.

5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and maintain the application, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You may request deletion of information associated with your use of the application as described below.

6. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of personal information associated with movieClipPosting by contacting us at the email address below. Please include enough information for us to identify your request. We will review and process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by movieClipPosting. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

movieClipPosting is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
